About InterCube

One partner.
Complete responsibility.

InterCube is an integrated interior solutions company specializing in design, engineering, manufacturing and execution of premium interior and architectural solutions. With world-class European machinery, skilled professionals and a passion for precision, we deliver spaces that are beautiful, functional and built to last.

  • Creative design with functional planning & 3D visualization
  • Detailed engineering BOQs, shop drawings, value engineering
  • In-house advanced manufacturing with strict quality control
  • Professional installation, project management & after-sales support
